Personality and audience

  • Who decides: owners and admins of online Quran, Arabic and Islamic-studies academies. Not students, and not individual tutors.
  • It should feel like: a serious, organised operations platform with warmth and cultural fluency. Calm and precise.
  • Name: إتقان means mastery, doing a thing with precision and care. Most concepts build on that idea.
  • Must work in: Arabic (RTL) and English, light and dark, and in the dashboard sidebar and the favicon at 16px.

Market research, in short

  • Tutoring SaaS logos are mostly mascots (TutorBird's bird, TutorCruncher's dinosaur) or rounded lowercase wordmarks (Teachable, Teachworks, Kajabi, Wise).
  • Category colours are blue, violet, teal or acid. No competitor checked uses deep green with gold, or any Arabic mark.
  • The strongest SaaS systems (Stripe, Linear, Asana) own one simple shape that works in one colour at 16px.
  • Caution: cream, gold and Fraunces are also common "AI default" choices, so the mark's shape has to carry the distinctiveness.
  • TutorHamster could not be verified (site returned an SSL error; no search results). Full notes are in competitor-research.md.

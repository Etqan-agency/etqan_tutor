## Why each scored as it did

- **A:** the safest step. It keeps today's equity and finally reads at 16px. Eight-point stars are common among Islamic-education brands, so it is not very distinctive.
- **B:** the most refined typography. But a lowercase wordmark is the most crowded look among competitors (Teachable, Teachworks, Kajabi, Wise).
- **C:** the strongest pure-SaaS mark: compact, ownable, and readable as ET at 16px. It carries less education or cultural meaning.
- **D:** the crispest at tiny sizes. Bars and steps are also used by analytics products, so its meaning is soft.
- **E:** a good platform story and app icon. Four-part symmetric marks are a familiar genre.
- **F:** clean and friendly, but a geometric "e" is very common.
- **G:** unique in the category and truly bilingual. **Risk:** ت with two dots can read as a smiling face, especially at 16px. Test it with owners.
- **H:** matches the arch photography already on the site and feels institutional. At 16px the opening narrows. Keep it architectural rather than mosque-like as it is refined.

## Recommendation (your decision)

1. **H, Gateway Arch.** It fits the current site best and gives the premium, trustworthy institution feel.
2. **C, ET Ligature.** Choose it if Etqan should read first as a software platform.
3. **G, Ta & Nuqta.** The most distinctive option. Validate the "smiley" reading before choosing it.

A fallback with the least risk is **A**. It is a direct upgrade of today's logo.

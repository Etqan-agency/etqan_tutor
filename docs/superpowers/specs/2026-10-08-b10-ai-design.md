## 1. Goal

Give an academy's office TutorHamster's AI help where P1 saw it: a "generate" action next to the text fields
of articles, article categories, courses and employment contracts, and a monthly progress report per student
written from the academy's own records. The model is Claude, reached through the academy's own Claude
account or Etqan's default (D42). Every feature is a per-academy switch, off by default (PO-5), and nothing
the AI writes is saved until a person accepts it.

## 8. B10a rules and services

### 8.1 Services (`etqan.ai.services`)

- `start_draft(*, user, task, language, inputs) -> AiDraft` — validates (A-10/A-12), resolves (409), prunes
  (A-8), creates, queues on commit.
- `draft_for(user, draft_id) -> AiDraft` — 404 unless the requester; applies and persists the stale rule (A-6).
- `run_draft(draft_id)` — the worker body (called by the Celery task inside `academy_context`).
- `meter(resolved, draft)` — the AI-9 seam.
- `register_task(...)` is **not** offered: B7 and others ask B10 for a registry entry by request.
- Two optional registry hooks (`Task.validate(inputs)`, run by `start_draft` after cutting and before
  resolving, may raise a field 400; `Task.build(task, *, language, academy, inputs) -> Prompt`, run by the
  worker instead of the generic prompt builder, may query) let a task check its inputs early and compute
  server-side facts (B10b). B10a's eight tasks set neither.

### 8.2 B10a tasks

| Code | Inputs (kept to chars) | Output | max_chars | max_tokens | effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `article.summary` | title 160, category 80, body 20 000, current 300 | text | 300 | 2 150 | low |
| `article.body` | title 160, category 80, summary 300, current 20 000 | html | 20 000 | 12 000 | medium |
| `article.seo_title` | title 160, summary 300, body 20 000 | text | 60 | 2 030 | low |
| `article.seo_description` | title 160, summary 300, body 20 000 | text | 160 | 2 080 | low |
| `article.keywords` | title 160, summary 300, body 20 000 | text (comma-separated) | 255 | 2 127 | low |
| `article_category.description` | name 80, current 300 | text | 300 | 2 150 | low |
| `course.description` | name 120, course language, current 5 000 | text | 5 000 | 4 500 | low |
| `contract.details` | teacher name 150, reference 60, current 10 000 | text | 10 000 | 7 000 | medium |

Every input is optional text. **Over-long inputs are cut, never refused**: HTML inputs (`body`, the article
`current`) are first reduced to text (tags stripped, entities decoded), then every input is cut to its limit
at a whole word. A 400 is only for an unknown task, an unknown input key or a non-text value (field-keyed).
A result longer than `max_chars` is cut at the last whole word within it and marked `truncated` (before
`to_html` for html tasks). The contract task sends the teacher's display name and the contract's own text
(needed in a contract's wording; free text per AI-8).

## 11. B10a risks

1. **A key in a response or log** — the integrations rules hold (secrets never serialised); `complete()` never
   logs the key or the prompt.
2. **A worker in the wrong academy** — the task takes `schema_name` and runs in `academy_context`; a test
   queues in one academy and checks the draft lands there.
3. **Model HTML reaching a page** — A-11 plus `clean_html` on save.
4. **Runaway cost** — throttle (A-9), per-task `max_tokens`, and Etqan's default only while a switch is on.

### M03-R3-001 Admin home (AR): the empty-month lines read "after this month", and the card titles/estimate are literal
- Steps: sign in as `qa-admin`, Arabic, 1440x900, open `/app/` early in the month, before any payments are in.
- Screenshot: `screens/r3/M03/M03-R3-001-empty-month-wording.png`
- Expected: natural Arabic, for example "لا توجد دفعات حتى الآن هذا الشهر.", a title like "المالية" or "الملخص المالي", and "بأسعار الصرف الحالية".
- Actual:
  - "لا دفعات بعد هذا الشهر.", "لا مصروفات بعد هذا الشهر.", "لا تبرعات بعد هذا الشهر." and "لا إيرادات ولا مصروفات بعد هذا الشهر." are word for word from "…yet this month". To an Arabic reader "بعد هذا الشهر" means "after this month".
  - The card title is "المال" (EN "Money").
  - The net-profit estimate says "≈ … تقديريًا بأسعارك الحالية (بتاريخ …)": "your rates" where exchange rates are meant.
  - Keys: `billing.json` noRevenue/title; `finance.json` noExpenses/noProfit/noDonations/value/valueNoDate.
- Console/network: none.

### SMK-M06-01: EN subscription archive at 1440, the "Delete permanently" button is cut off at the table edge (P3, layout)
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign in as admin, EN, at 1440x900.
  2. Open `/app/scheduling/subscriptions/archive`.
- **Screenshot:** `screens/r3/smoke/M05-M06/SMK-M06-01-archive-en-1440-delete-cut.png`
- **Expected:** every column fits at 1440, as it does in AR, where "حذف نهائي" fits.
- **Actual:**
  - The table wrapper (`div.relative.overflow-x-auto`) has `scrollWidth` 1181 against `clientWidth`
    1126.
  - Each row's "Delete permanently" button spans x 1308–1458 inside a viewport 1440 px wide, so it
    shows as "Delete perman…".
  - You have to scroll sideways inside the card to reach it.
- **Likely cause:** the longer EN label on the outlined button (QA-1064 changed it to
  `destructiveOutline`) plus the nowrap columns. The Course and Teacher cells already wrap onto 2
  lines.
- **Console/network:** none.

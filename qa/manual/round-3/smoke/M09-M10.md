## Problems

### M09-SMOKE-001: Payslip page scrolls sideways at 375 when the payment note has a long unbroken word
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign in as admin in AR or EN at 375x812.
  2. Open `/app/payroll/payslips/11` (PAY-000011, Paid). Its payment note contains a long run of `|` with no
     spaces.
- **Screenshots:**
  - `screens/r3/smoke/M09-M10/M09-SMOKE-001-payslip-note-overflow-en-375.png`: scrollWidth 641 at 375.
  - `screens/r3/smoke/M09-M10/M09-SMOKE-001-payslip-note-overflow-ar-375.png`: scrollWidth 513 at 375. The whole
    RTL page is shifted, the header and the facts are cut off on the right, and the title and the back link are
    out of view.
- **Expected:** the note wraps inside the card, as it does at 1440 (`M09-payslip11-en-1440.png`), and the page
  does not scroll sideways.
- **Actual:**
  - The unbroken run sets the width of the facts grid.
  - The card and the page grow past the viewport.
- **Likely cause:**
  - In `dashboard/src/features/payroll/PayslipPage.tsx` (around line 307), the notes
    `<dd className="whitespace-pre-line break-words">` sits in a grid cell.
  - `break-words` (`overflow-wrap: break-word`) does not lower the min-content width of a grid item.
  - A fix would use `[overflow-wrap:anywhere]` and/or `min-w-0` on the cell, which is the pattern the chat bubble
    fix (QA-1450 series) uses.
  - This is related to ETQAN-QA-1212 (a long reason stretched the Adjustments table). The payslip notes cell
    was not covered by that fix.
- **Console/network:** none.

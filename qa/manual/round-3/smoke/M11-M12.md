### SMOKE-M12-01 «إضافة دور» / "Add role" and «إضافة كود» / "Add code" have no "+" icon
- **Steps:** Sign in as qa-admin. Open `/app/settings/roles`, then `/app/settings/registration-codes`, in AR and in EN at 1440 (light or dark).
- **Screens:**
  - `screens/r3/smoke/M11-M12/SMOKE-M12-01-roles-add-no-plus-ar.png`
  - `screens/r3/smoke/M11-M12/SMOKE-M12-01-codes-add-no-plus-en.png`
  - Same at 375: `M12-ar-375-settings-roles.png`, `M12-ar-375-settings-registration-codes.png`.
- **Expected:** The product rule is "every add button carries the '+'" (`decisions.md`, ETQAN-QA-1009 / 1039 / 1531). The M11 pages and the catalogue all follow it.
- **Actual:**
  - Measured in the DOM: «إضافة دور» svg=0 and «إضافة كود» svg=0 (EN "Add role" and "Add code" likewise).
  - The Roles button is also the small size (`buttonVariants({size:"sm"})` in `dashboard/src/features/access/RolesList.tsx:116-122`). So it is shorter than the filter beside it and than the add buttons on other pages (it sits about 4 px lower than the «أي حالة» select).
- **Console/network:** none.
- **Suggested severity:** P4, spacing/icon (consistency).

### SMOKE-M12-02 (note, not a fail) The Security log shows Arabic names in a different order in EN
- **Steps:** EN, `/app/settings/security-log`, at 1440 or 375.
- **Screen:** `screens/r3/smoke/M11-M12/SMOKE-M12-02-security-log-person-order-en.png`; phone `M12-en-375-settings-security-log.png`.
- **What you see:**
  - The Person column reads «(Hamza Al-Qadi) حمزة القاضي», because each name is wrapped in `<bdi>` (`SecurityLogPage.tsx:105,111`).
  - Other EN pages show the same kind of name as «عمر الشريف (Omar Al-Sharif)»: Certificates, Homework, Reports.
- **Assessment:** This is technically correct bidi isolation, and nothing overlaps. It is only an inconsistency in how bilingual names look across EN pages. It is left for triage to decide, and it does not fail the area.
